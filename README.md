## 📌 Project Overview

The **OT Report Dashboard** provides a centralized view of overtime and employee compliance metrics. It enables users to quickly analyze OT hours, identify compliant and non-compliant employees, and compare overtime across departments.

## ✨ Dashboard Features

- 📊 Compliance and non-compliance percentage KPIs
- 🏢 Department-level filtering

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to provide management and operations teams with a simple, interactive way to:

1. Monitor overtime utilization.
2. Track employee OT compliance.
3. Identify departments with higher OT hours.
4. Compare compliant and non-compliant employees.
5. Support data-driven workforce and operational decisions.
